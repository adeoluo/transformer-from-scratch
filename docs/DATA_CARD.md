# Data Card - Transformer From Scratch

## Source
- **Fully synthetic.** Sequences are generated in-process by a seeded random generator. There
  is no external dataset, download, or network access.

## Composition
- Each sequence is a random body of `length` symbols drawn from an alphabet of `n_symbols`,
  followed by a separator token (id 0), followed by the same body again.
- Default task: 4 symbols, length 5, so the vocabulary is 5 ids and the sequence length is 11.
- Default splits: 64 training and 64 held-out sequences, disjoint by `(body)` construction.

## Labels and supervision
- The training signal is next-token prediction; there are no human labels.
- The copy region (the second half) is the part that requires attention; it is scored separately.

## Provenance and licensing
- Generated by this repository's code under the MIT license. No third-party data is included.

## Filtering and contamination
- Generation rejects duplicates, and the test split excludes any sequence present in the
  training split, so the two are disjoint.

## Known limitations
- The alphabet and length are deliberately small so the task is exactly solvable and fast. The
  distribution is not meant to resemble natural language.
